About the work

Blaine Mooers is an RNA crystallographer interested in how molecular structure shapes biological function. The work combines careful crystallization, diffraction, and structural analysis to reveal the architecture of RNA and the interactions that make it dynamic.

RNA is not just a message between DNA and protein. It is a molecule with shape, motion, and purpose.

Current focus

Fold shapes function

Blaine Mooers's work explores the relationship between RNA folds and biological behavior—particularly how local conformational changes enable recognition, regulation, and catalysis.

RNA structures reveal more than static coordinates. They provide a map of binding pockets, helical motifs, flexible regions, and molecular contacts that guide experiments and deepen our understanding of cellular regulation.
